Help Topics

Reporting an Issue
How to pick a category and describe what's wrong.
Read guide
Scroll to "What's the problem?" and tap the tile closest to your issue. Each category is pre-mapped to a GVMC department, so your letter reaches the right desk automatically — no need to guess who handles what.
Location & GPS
Auto-detecting your address or entering it manually.
Read guide
Tap "Detect my location" and allow the permission prompt — Netiv fills in your GPS coordinates and address automatically. If GPS fails or is unavailable, use the manual fields for house number, area, landmark, city, and pincode instead.
Photo Evidence
Attaching a photo to support your complaint.
Read guide
Tap the photo zone to take or upload a picture. Clear, well-lit photos that show the full extent of the issue get faster action — try to include a nearby landmark or signage in the frame if possible.
Your Letter
How the official letter is generated and formatted.
Read guide
Tapping "Generate official letter" drafts a formal complaint addressed to the GVMC Commissioner through the correct department, with your description, photo status, and location already filled in — ready to send.
Sending It
Email, WhatsApp, or helpline — pick what's easiest.
Read guide
Once your letter is ready, send it by email or WhatsApp to GVMC's official grievance channels, or call the toll-free helpline directly for urgent issues. Browsers can't attach the photo automatically yet — attach it manually before sending.
Account & Login
Managing your Netiv account, email, and phone number.
Read guide
Register with your email, phone number, and a password to save your details for future reports. Once logged in, Netiv can pre-fill your name and phone number so you don't retype them every time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit a complaint?
  1. Tap a category under "What's the problem?"
  2. Upload a photo and detect your location
  3. Describe the issue and add your details
  4. Generate the official letter
  5. Send it by email, WhatsApp, or call the helpline
Why isn't my GPS working?
Check that location permission is allowed for this site in your browser settings, and that GPS is turned on. If it still fails, use the manual address fields instead — house number, area, landmark, city, and pincode.
Can I edit my complaint after generating the letter?
Yes — just update the description, photo, or location fields and tap "Generate official letter" again. It will rebuild the letter with your latest details.
How long does GVMC take to respond?
Response times vary by department and issue severity. For urgent matters, calling the toll-free helpline directly usually gets the fastest attention.
